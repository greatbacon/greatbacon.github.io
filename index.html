The Shadows Institute was founded as an experimental space for implementing peer production models for horror fiction.

We seek to take back the power of the pen & purse from the short sighted suits and put it back in the hands of the creators.

Our primary publication is currently a quarterly zine, "Shadows of the Rocky Mountains", which focuses on small, folk horror stories set in and around the area.
